Tiles follow the road as it curves. Tap a burgundy tile to open its puzzle.

Puzzle tile 1

Crown Puzzle

Years of school lead here — one last test before your first real job.

Place a crown in some cells so that every row, every column and every coloured region contains exactly one crown, and no two crowns touch — not even diagonally. Click a cell to place or remove a crown. This edition runs 7 rows by 7 columns with 7 regions.

Puzzle tile 2

Sun & Moon

Two lives, one path — say "I do," and figure out the balance together.

Fill every empty cell with a sun ☀️ or a moon 🌙. Each row and column must end up with an equal number of suns and moons, and no three matching symbols may sit in a row or column. This board is twice the size of before, and now only about a third of the cells are given, so it's a bit tighter than it first looks.

Puzzle tile 3

Connect the Numbers

The big promotion is one connection away — climb the ladder, one link at a time.

Draw a single path from 1 to 6 that visits every cell of this 7×7 board exactly once. Click cell 1 to start, then keep clicking a cell next to your last step to extend the trail. The numbers must come up in the correct order. The route this time has no clean shape to latch onto — it isn't a spiral, and it doesn't just sweep side to side or up and down — so you'll want to feel your way through it.

Puzzle tile 4

Patchwork

Retirement at last — time to sit back and see how all the pieces fit together.

Cover this 4×10 board completely with the ten coloured pieces — nothing may overlap and no gap may remain. Click a piece in the tray to select it, rotate it if needed, then click the board cell where its highlighted corner should land. Click a placed piece to pick it back up. There are no simple squares in this set — it's a trickier mix of straight, L, J, T, S and Z shapes that interlock in less obvious ways.
